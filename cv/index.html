James R. Valcourt

Computational drug discovery · New York, New York

  • jrvalcourt@gmail.com

Summary

Interdisciplinary researcher in computational drug discovery, combining physics-based simulation, machine learning, and AI agents with hands-on work at every stage of small-molecule programs, from inception through IND. Earlier work spans molecular dynamics of GPCRs and antibodies, stem cell fate control, and writing about biology for general audiences.

Experience

D. E. Shaw Research

2020 – present

Drug Discovery Scientist (Senior Member of the Technical Staff) · New York, NY

  • Carry several small-molecule programs at a time, each with one or two other scientists, from target assessment and hit finding through lead optimization, candidate selection, and IND-enabling studies.
  • Work across structural rationale, medicinal chemistry strategy, DMPK, and preclinical development rather than within a single function.
  • Apply long-timescale molecular dynamics, free energy perturbation, virtual screening and docking, machine learning, and LLM-based agents to targets and binding modes that conventional approaches have not cracked.
  • Design and direct in vitro and in vivo work at CRO partners to test computational predictions and close the design–make–test cycle.
  • Served as internal lead for one of several programs run in collaboration with Relay Therapeutics.
